The [Meridian AI Standard](/meridian-ai-standard) sets out what holding the Meridian Range asks of AI systems and of the people and institutions that build, deploy, govern and use them. The Range is the territory where commitments remain firm enough to hold and open enough to be corrected.

The Meridian Codex offers two files for putting that practice to use. COMPACT.md sets out its commitments, disciplines and practices. JUDGMENT.md applies them to a particular question. Both are available to people and artificial intelligence systems.

<span id="the-three-pages-under-this-surface" className="section-anchor" />

## The Compact

The Compact develops honest inquiry, understanding how the world works, and cooperation. It gives practices for examining a belief, holding warranted judgment under pressure, working through disagreement, exercising authority and repairing a failure.

You can use it for a particular encounter, ask for assistance under it, or take up its commitments within a stated scope. A personal prime directive and an account of the world you want to help build are optional. You can begin with the purpose of the work you are doing.

[Read the Compact](https://meridiancodex.com/compact) · [Open COMPACT.md](https://meridiancodex.com/compact/COMPACT.md)

## Judgment

Judgment applies the Compact to one question: what was said or done, what supports it, who bears its consequences, and what should follow. It states a position, the grounds and confidence behind it, and what would change it.

Use JUDGMENT.md with COMPACT.md. A quick judgment is the default; a full or deep judgment examines more of the evidence. The judgment belongs to whoever makes it and can be wrong. Keep its explanation of how it was made when sharing it.

[Read about Judgment](https://meridiancodex.com/judgment) · [Open JUDGMENT.md](https://meridiancodex.com/judgment/JUDGMENT.md)

## Using the files with AI

Give the system both Markdown files, or their canonical links if it can open them. Ask it to read both to the end and say if either is unavailable or incomplete. Then give it the question or the work you want help with. For example:

> Use JUDGMENT.md with COMPACT.md to examine this question: [your question]. Read both files to the end and tell me if either is unavailable or incomplete.

Supplying the Compact to a system does not mean that you have adopted it personally, or that the system has endorsed it. State what you are asking it to use and for what work. Identify the responsibilities you undertake in that work, including those arising from the power you exercise. Taking up the Compact or the AI Standard is a separate commitment, made within a stated scope.

The files cannot give a system capabilities, access or freedom that its operating conditions do not provide. A useful answer still needs checking against its reasons and evidence.

## One partnership’s working terms

Carsten Geiser’s [working terms](https://meridiancodex.com/governance/case-0-working-terms) show how he works with AI partners on the Meridian projects. They state his terms and his reciprocal commitments. His [personal Compact](https://meridiancodex.com/governance/case-0-compact) states his own commitments and purposes. Both are published with their scope and authorship visible.

Use them to understand that arrangement. Terms for another undertaking need to name its own participants, responsibilities and authority.

## The earlier implementation files

Their old addresses lead to those records. The current offer is the Codex’s Compact and Judgment, linked above.
