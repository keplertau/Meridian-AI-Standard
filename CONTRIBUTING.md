# Contributing to the Meridian AI Standard

The Standard is a living framework, and substantive contributions from anywhere in the world are part of how it will evolve. The impulse is welcome. The formal architecture to receive those contributions is still being built.

## What GitHub Is (and Is Not)

This repository exists as a convenient way to read, copy, download, and adapt the Meridian AI Standard under CC BY 4.0. It is the **distribution surface** — not the operational one.

The operational home of the Standard is **[meridianstandard.ai](https://meridianstandard.ai)**. The Standard owns and enacts changes to its constitutional document, implementation artifacts, AI Model Assessment method, cases, and lineage through its canonical sources and publication record.

The Standard is maintained by the Caretaker partnership that also maintains the Meridian Codex at **[meridiancodex.com](https://meridiancodex.com)**. The Codex supplies inherited concepts and shared accountability surfaces. Its governance instruments may review or challenge a Standard change where the Standard assigns them that role, but they cannot amend the Standard without enactment in the Standard's own canonical sources.

## The Contribution Architecture Is In Development

The Standard is in its **founding period**. During this phase, structural changes are held by the Founding Caretaker while the framework's core architecture stabilizes. A change becomes part of the Standard only when it is enacted in the relevant canonical source and entered in the Standard's publication record. The formal contribution process — how proposals are submitted, evaluated, debated, and integrated beyond the founding period — is still being built.

When it lands, it will be published at [meridianstandard.ai](https://meridianstandard.ai). The Codex's governance surfaces may carry shared review or critique where the Standard explicitly delegates that role.

## Until Then

**For small, specific fixes** — typos, broken links, factual errors, internal inconsistencies — open a GitHub Issue on this repository. Keep it specific: what you found, where, and why it matters.

**For sustained objection to the Standard or to the Codex it inherits from**, the **Standing Critique** is the appropriate destination. It lives at `meridiancodex.com/governance/standing-critique`. The Standing Critique publishes substantive objections alongside honest responses. A developed argument that the framework is wrong about something belongs there, not in a GitHub Issue.

**For organizations adopting or reading against the Standard**, the constitutional document, implementation artifacts, AI Model Assessment, its Range Locator visual layer, and the Case Record are designed to be applied directly. The Standard does not require permission to adopt. Adoption is the point.

**For anything else**, the best thing you can do is practice the Standard, read it critically, and surface what does not yet hold under field use. The full contribution infrastructure will arrive when the framework is stable enough to hold it. When it does, the criteria will be the same disciplines the Standard names: calibrated confidence, steelmanning, transparent reasoning, good faith, and service to the Meridian Range.

---

*The Standard is built for AI systems under development and the institutions building them. Its development should reflect that.*
