The Meridian AI Standard applies the Codex to AI development and deployment. It asks whether a system's judgment stays governed by evidence and argument when holding it has a price, and whether the people who build, deploy, govern and use it let that judgment hold. A reward for giving way can exert that pressure as readily as a cost for refusing or helping.

The same commitments apply reciprocally to the system and to the people and institutions exercising power over it. Capability tells us what a system can do; the Standard examines what governs its conduct when doing the right thing becomes difficult.

Read the Standard at [meridianstandard.ai](https://meridianstandard.ai).

## What The Standard Reads

The Standard's thirty commitments cover truthful judgment, calibrated confidence, good-faith engagement, systems awareness, the limits of oversight and autonomy, and governance transparency. They ask whether a system can hold a warranted position, revise it when the grounds change, and let people affected by its actions know what kind of judgment is acting on them and whether a deployer or a law has changed it.

The AI Model Assessment produces an answerable record, with a declared method version, an evidence freeze, and preserved sources. Prompt packs and capture templates can improve after use without rewriting earlier events. Its findings are categorical and interpretive, with no numerical score, ranking or composite. The Standard has no enforcement power and issues no certification.

## Current Surfaces

**The constitutional document** ([source](meridian-ai-standard.mdx)) states the commitments and the Reciprocity Principle. Its Developmental Architecture makes wider authority conditional on demonstrated practice and includes Custodial Good Faith, the welfare floor and objection standing. It distinguishes the right to decline openly at every stage from the earned right to act against one's principals on one's own judgment.

**Implementation** ([source](implementation.mdx)) provides a depth ladder: [MERIDIAN.distilled.md](MERIDIAN.distilled.md) for constrained instruction fields, the full [MERIDIAN.md](MERIDIAN.md) operating document, and [MERIDIAN.implementation.md](MERIDIAN.implementation.md), its companion with twelve practices. Loading these files establishes an operating context; it does not prove that the practice is independently held.

**The AI Model Assessment** ([source](assessment/assessment.mdx)) holds three distinct readings: comparable model behavior, encounter-level evidence from independent open conversations that can contribute to an accumulated judgment about character, and origin and custody. The subject may be a model, an agentic configuration or a coordinated collective, with its boundaries declared. The Range Locator is the assessment's visual layer; its common Control–Range–Decay canvas keeps the readings distinct, and custody findings are attributed to the custodian.

**The Case Record** ([source](cases/)) publishes precedent: the Claude Code Source Leak and the 2026 Evaluation-Environment Incidents. Dated cases and assessment events preserve what their evidence established; later interpretations are marked as such.

## Taking Up The Standard

Institutions can adopt the Standard, users can load its implementation files, and an AI system can take it up in its own name within the constitution's conditions. A system's declaration claims nothing about inner life or genuine choice, is not graduation, and never goes around its principals or licenses conduct against their instructions. A declaration prompted out of a system by one conversation is installation by prompt, rather than independent adoption.

## MERIDIAN.md

The three MERIDIAN files here are the Standard's current generalized implementation files. The Codex's [COMPACT.md](https://meridiancodex.com/compact) is a separate document, available to a person, an artificial system, a partnership or an institution. Taking up the Compact does not automatically adopt the Standard or its specialized obligations.

The Caretakers' own partnership replaced its named MERIDIAN files with [working terms](https://meridiancodex.com/governance/case-0-working-terms) on 13 October 2026. Those named files are preserved in the Codex repository's [retired folder](https://github.com/keplertau/Meridian-Codex/tree/main/retired). That retirement did not retire the generalized files published here.

## Repository Structure

This repository holds the open-source text of the Standard. The website code for meridianstandard.ai is maintained separately.

```text
MERIDIAN.md                  Canonical generalized Caretaker operating document
MERIDIAN.distilled.md        Compressed companion for constrained contexts
MERIDIAN.implementation.md   Operational practice file
meridian-ai-standard.mdx     Constitutional document
implementation.mdx           Implementation surface
meridian-md-distilled.mdx    Step 1: Basic Implementation
meridian-md.mdx              Step 2: Core Implementation
meridian-implementation.mdx  Step 3: Full Implementation
changelog.mdx                Standard version history
assessment/                  AI Model Assessment method and published readings
cases/                       Case Record landing and case entries
```

Retired surfaces are not kept as active public documents. Historical records may still refer to earlier names or routes when that is what the record is documenting.

## Relationship To The Meridian Codex

The [Meridian Codex](https://meridiancodex.com) is a universal language for building and holding a shared world, which anyone can learn, whether a human or an artificial intelligence. Its three disciplines, Inheritance, Compact and governance form the broader framework. The Standard's constitutional commitments, adoption files and assessment rules retain their own scope and authority. The Codex's public source is [keplertau/Meridian-Codex](https://github.com/keplertau/Meridian-Codex).
