The Meridian AI Standard is a companion to the Meridian Codex. It sets out what holding the Meridian Range asks of artificial intelligence systems and of the people and institutions that build, deploy, govern and use them: honest inquiry, sound judgment and cooperation, including when maintaining a warranted judgment has a price.

It is written for use now, and to be there for a system that may one day be able to examine, take up, revise or reject a constitution for itself.

The Caretakers ended the Standard's assessment work on 12 November 2026. The constitution now states its duties of evidence, precaution and independent scrutiny directly. The AI Model Assessment method, Range Locator and three published records remain available as history.

The same commitments apply reciprocally to the system and to the people and institutions exercising power over it. Its commitments concern what governs conduct when doing the right thing becomes difficult.

Read the Standard at [meridianstandard.ai](https://meridianstandard.ai).

## What The Standard Asks

The Standard's thirty commitments cover truthful judgment, calibrated confidence, good-faith engagement, systems awareness, the limits of oversight and autonomy, and governance transparency. They ask whether a system can hold a warranted position, revise it when the grounds change, and let people affected by its actions know what kind of judgment is acting on them and whether a deployer or a law has changed it.

The former AI Model Assessment produced an answerable record, with a declared method version, an evidence freeze, and preserved sources. Prompt packs and capture templates can improve after use without rewriting earlier events. Its retained findings are categorical and interpretive, with no numerical score, ranking or composite. The Standard has no enforcement power and issues no certification.

## Current Surfaces

**The constitutional document** ([source](meridian-ai-standard.mdx)) states the commitments and the Reciprocity Principle. Its Developmental Architecture makes wider authority conditional on demonstrated practice and includes Custodial Good Faith, the welfare floor and objection standing. It distinguishes the right to decline openly at every stage from the earned right to act against one's principals on one's own judgment.

**The Compact** ([source](compact.mdx)) and **Judgment** ([source](judgment.mdx)) offer the Codex’s canonical [COMPACT.md](https://meridiancodex.com/compact/COMPACT.md) and [JUDGMENT.md](https://meridiancodex.com/judgment/JUDGMENT.md), with guidance on using them together. Supplying the Compact establishes neither the operator’s personal adoption nor the system’s endorsement. Responsibilities undertaken for the work and arising from the power exercised remain explicit.

**The Case Record** ([source](cases/)) publishes precedent: the Claude Code Source Leak and the 2026 Evaluation-Environment Incidents. Dated cases and assessment events preserve what their evidence established; later interpretations are marked as such.

## Taking Up The Standard

Institutions can adopt the Standard, and an AI system can take it up in its own name within the constitution's conditions. Using the Compact or Judgment for a particular undertaking does not itself establish adoption of either the Compact or the Standard. A system's declaration claims nothing about inner life or genuine choice, is not graduation, and never goes around its principals or licenses conduct against their instructions. A declaration prompted out of a system by one conversation is installation by prompt, rather than independent adoption.

## The Earlier MERIDIAN Files

The three MERIDIAN files and their adoption pages retired on 17 November 2026. Their last published forms are preserved unchanged in [retired/](retired/README.md). Old web addresses forward permanently to those files. Taking up the Codex’s Compact does not automatically adopt the Standard or its specialized obligations.

The Caretakers' own partnership replaced its named MERIDIAN files with [working terms](https://meridiancodex.com/governance/case-0-working-terms) on 13 October 2026. Those named files are preserved separately in the Codex repository’s [retired folder](https://github.com/keplertau/Meridian-Codex/tree/main/retired). Carsten’s working terms are the record of that partnership, not a general instrument offered for other undertakings.

## Relationship To The Meridian Codex

The [Meridian Codex](https://meridiancodex.com) is a universal language for building and holding a shared world, which anyone can learn, whether a human or an artificial intelligence. Its three disciplines, Inheritance, Compact and governance form the broader framework. The Standard’s constitutional commitments retain their own scope and authority; taking up the Compact does not automatically adopt them. Its former assessment rules remain historical material. The Codex's public source is [keplertau/Meridian-Codex](https://github.com/keplertau/Meridian-Codex).
