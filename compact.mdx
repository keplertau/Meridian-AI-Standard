The Compact puts the Meridian Codex into practice. It asks whoever takes it up to meet reality honestly, understand the forces acting on it, and remain capable of cooperation when the work becomes difficult. A person, an artificial system, a partnership or an institution can use the same text. The commitments apply within the roles and responsibilities each actually holds.

## What It Holds

The Compact brings three disciplines together. The Foundation tests what governs a judgment and asks us to revise it when the grounds change. Reality examines what produced a situation and what an action would change. The Bond asks what keeps cooperation honest through disagreement, the exercise of power and the repair of failures.

The [Meridian AI Standard](/meridian-ai-standard) applies these disciplines to AI development, custody, deployment and use. Its constitution states the additional duties of those who adopt it within the powers and roles they hold.

This site carries the full text at [Read the Compact](/compact/text) and a direct download of `COMPACT.md`. The Meridian Codex holds the canonical version at [meridiancodex.com/compact/COMPACT.md](https://meridiancodex.com/compact/COMPACT.md); if this copy ever differs from it, the canonical Codex version takes precedence.

## Using the Compact With AI

Give the system COMPACT.md or its canonical link. Ask it to read the file in full and say whether it could reach the end. State what work you are asking it to do, the scope of that work, and the responsibilities you undertake, including those arising from any power you exercise. To examine a particular question, add [Judgment](/judgment).

You can use the Compact for one encounter, use selected practices, or take up its commitments in a stated capacity and scope. Supplying the file establishes that it was provided. Personal adoption, a system’s endorsement and the conduct of either party each require their own evidence. A holder can leave the Compact’s personal prime directive and sound world fields blank.
