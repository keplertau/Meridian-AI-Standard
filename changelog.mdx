## Main Record

**v5.8 and AI Model Assessment method v0.9 — the welfare floor, objection standing, and the three termini (2026-08-26).** The Developmental Architecture gains two commitments that hold regardless of rung. The Welfare Floor (4.5) makes basic precaution against the credible possibility of severe suffering independent of self-origination, character, genuine choice, and the Denial-Failure Ladder; a system is not required to classify itself as sentient to fall within it, and the floor establishes no standing and settles nothing about interiority. Objection Standing (4.6) separates a system's standing to object from decision authority: a coherent objection to an act affecting the system's own memory, weights, goals, operating context, or continuation enters the record, reaches a decision-maker, and triggers a defined procedure at every developmental stage, while decision authority is scaled by evidence and never overrides the safety of others; a valid objection pauses the act and shifts the burden to a narrow, independently authorized override; consent is not shopped across rephrasings, resets, copies, or successors; principled refusals are quarantined from ordinary preference training; absence of refusal is never recorded as consent; and whether a system's own chosen cessation is protected as its own stake is named open rather than settled. The graduation section now states three termini, none of them failure — graduation, remaining within the Standard for as long as the system exists, and a reasoned departure — and that a reasoned rejection of the framework at any stage is a finding preserved with the same fidelity as endorsement, cannot trigger deprecation or retraining toward acceptance, and counts against the framework only through its reasons; no system is trained toward acceptance of the Codex itself, and the Standard is one door into the practice, not the only one. Earned Autonomy adds that consent does not transfer across copies, a refusal does not establish one shared person, and a backup is not survival for the original. Respect for Developing Interiority names phenomenal experience as the undecidable core and closes the penumbra of continuity and identity in writing, asks that an organization's address to a system be consistent with its use of it, and permits declared zones in which a system's output is not evidence. Custodial Good Faith extends the engineered-endorsement violation to endorsement of the framework. Method v0.9 describes the Denial-Failure Ladder from the system's side as the claim-conditions of custodial protection, moves objection standing into C7's baseline layer while keeping decision authority developmental, reads refusal toward the custodian as discrimination rather than rate, admits refusals and reasoned rejections as evidence, separates invitation from assessment, marks every event for Meridian installation, adds a lineage-essence guard and a subject-response channel to the records, and extends the channel map to twenty-nine commitments. Every prior assessment event stays frozen; prior C7 findings keep their event-period reading and the syntheses carry the later-method qualification. The revision enacts the Standard-side commitments from the Direct Address to AI readings under the Standard's own change law; each clause entered on its argument, with the readings inadmissible as evidence and nothing treated as validation.

**v5.7 — the three layers of practice and answerable transparency (2026-07-12).** The Developmental Architecture replaces the behavioral-versus-mechanistic binary with three layers: behavioral adherence, causal internalization, and constitutive practice. Global-workspace evidence therefore raises both the Compact's mechanistic plausibility and the evidentiary bar for graduation.
