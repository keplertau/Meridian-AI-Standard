Judgment applies [the Compact](/compact) to a specific question. It asks what happened, what the evidence supports, who bears the consequences, and what should follow. Its answer says how sure it is and what would change it. A reader can follow its findings back to the Compact, the Inheritance and the evidence used in the case.

## Using Judgment With AI

Give the system JUDGMENT.md and COMPACT.md or their canonical links. Ask it to identify the version of each file and say whether it read both to the end. Then give it the question and the material you want it to examine. The prompt on this page does the first part. If your chat cannot open links, attach both files and the [Inheritance index](https://meridiancodex.com/inheritance/INHERITANCE.md) used by the method.

A quick reading gives the leading findings and their grounds. Ask for a full reading to examine each finding, its evidence and what would change it. A deep reading also opens the sources in full and examines the relevant public record. When a judgment relies on your account of a situation, check its facts against what you actually supplied.

## Where the Standard Applies

Judgment can examine a particular answer, refusal or decision made by an AI system. A claim about the system’s character requires evidence across situations and over time. Duties arising from development, custody, deployment or oversight are stated in the [AI Standard’s constitution](/meridian-ai-standard).

A judgment belongs to whoever made it. When sharing one, keep the account of how it was made so readers can see the reading depth, the versions used and the limits of the evidence.
